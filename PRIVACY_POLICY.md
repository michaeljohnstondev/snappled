# Snappled Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: April 15, 2026**

Big Vibe Studios ("we", "our", "us") operates the Snappled mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- **Email address** — used for account creation and authentication
- **Username** — displayed publicly on your profile and content

### User-Generated Content (Optional)
- **Videos** — short video "snapples" you choose to record and upload
- **Audio** — captured as part of video recordings
- **Prompts** — text prompts you create for the community

### Automatically Collected
- **Device identifiers** — used for authentication and security
- **Usage data** — in-app actions such as games played, purchases made, and content interactions

## How We Use Your Information

- **App functionality** — to provide core features including account management, content creation, game matchmaking, and the in-app economy
- **Security** — to prevent fraud, detect abuse, and protect user accounts
- **Improvements** — to understand how features are used and improve the app experience

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services, including:
- Firebase Authentication (account credentials)
- Cloud Firestore (user profiles, game data, content metadata)
- Firebase Storage (uploaded videos)

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States.

## In-App Economy

Snappled includes a virtual economy with coins, tickets, and trophies. These virtual items have no real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged for real currency. In-app purchases allow you to buy virtual coins and tickets.

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal information. We do **not** share your data with third-party advertisers. Your data is only processed by our service providers (Google Firebase) for the purpose of operating the app.

## Children's Privacy

Snappled is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Rights

You may:
- **View** your profile data within the app
- **Edit** your username and profile information
- **Delete** your content (snapples, prompts) within the app
- **Request account deletion** by contacting us at the email below

Upon account deletion, we will remove your personal information, profile data, and associated content. Some anonymized data (such as game statistics) may be retained.

## Data Retention

- Account data is retained while your account is active
- Deleted content is removed from our servers
- If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed within 30 days

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or via email.
