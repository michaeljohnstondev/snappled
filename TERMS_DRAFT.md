- Creators receive a percentage of each sale (70-90% based on level)

## 6. Creator Rewards
- Creators earn coins when their snapples are purchased
- Creators earn topic tickets when their snapples are promoted (survive prompt expiry)
- Creator royalty percentage scales with account level
- Rewards are in virtual currency only — not redeemable for real money

- Creators may delete their own snapples at any time

- Refunds are tracked in an internal ledger for transparency
- Refunds are issued in virtual currency (coins), not real money
- The refund amount is the price each buyer originally paid
