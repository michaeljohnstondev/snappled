Delete Your Snappled Account

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

You can delete your Snappled account yourself, from inside the app, and it takes effect immediately. There is no form to fill in and nobody to wait on.

Delete It From The App

Open Snappled and go to Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. You will be shown exactly what is about to be erased, asked to type your username, and asked to confirm it is you. Your account is closed the moment you confirm and you are signed out immediately.

If You Cannot Get Into The App

If you have lost access to your account, email us from the address it was registered with and we will do it for you:

Email A Deletion Request

What Gets Deleted

  • Account information: Email address, username, profile and sign-in
  • Your videos: Every snapple you recorded, and the video files behind them
  • Comments and votes: Everything you wrote or rated
  • Economy data: Coins, tickets, trophies, deck and achievements
  • Social data: Followers, following, likes, wishlists, and your name in other people's lists

What Does Not Get Deleted

Two things survive, and neither identifies you:

  • Prompts you wrote. The text of a game prompt belongs to everyone playing and may be live in the current season. The prompt stays; your name comes off it.
  • Purchase records. We keep a record of what was charged, stripped of everything but an anonymous id and an amount, because we are required to keep financial records.

Your Snapples, If People Bought Them

If other players have spent coins on your snapples, you will be asked what should happen to them.

  • Delete them (the default) — the videos are erased and everyone who bought one is refunded the coins they paid.
  • Leave them in the game — the people who bought them keep the cards they paid for, with your name removed. Everything else about your account still goes.

That choice is yours and it is never made for you. Your videos are only kept if you say so.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your content, progress and virtual currency are lost. Anything bought with real money is not refunded — refunds for purchases are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

How Long It Takes

Your account is closed and you are locked out immediately. The data itself is erased in the background, normally within minutes and always within 30 days.
