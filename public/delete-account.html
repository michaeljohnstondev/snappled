What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently removed within 30 days:

  • Account information: Email address, username, profile data
  • Created content: All snapples (videos) you uploaded
  • Prompts: Any prompts you created
  • Game history: Match results, scores, and statistics
  • Economy data: Coins, tickets, trophies, purchase history
  • Achievements: All earned achievements and progress
  • Deck: Your card collection and deck configuration
  • Social data: Followers, following, likes, and wishlists

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your content, progress, purchases, and virtual currency will be permanently lost. Any in-app purchases will not be refunded.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. You will receive an email confirmation once your account and data have been deleted.
